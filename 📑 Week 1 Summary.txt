📑 Week 1 Summary – Time-Series Preprocessing & Decomposition

## Objective
The primary focus of Week 1 was to establish a solid foundation for the demand forecasting pipeline by preparing and decomposing the time-series dataset. This ensures clean, structured data for accurate forecasting and anomaly detection in subsequent weeks.

---

## Activities Completed

### 1. Dataset Acquisition
- Collected historical supply chain data containing **daily sales and inventory logs**.
- Verified data integrity (checked for missing dates, duplicate entries, and inconsistent formats).

### 2. Time-Series Indexing
- Converted the `date` column into a **datetime index** using Pandas.
- Ensured chronological ordering of records for accurate time-series analysis.

### 3. Resampling & Aggregation
- Resampled daily data into **weekly and monthly aggregates** to smooth fluctuations.
- Created multiple views (daily, weekly, monthly) for flexible analysis depending on stakeholder needs.

### 4. Handling Missing Values
- Identified gaps in the time-series (missing dates).
- Applied **interpolation techniques** (linear and forward-fill) to impute missing values.
- Documented the chosen method to maintain transparency in preprocessing.

### 5. Time-Series Decomposition
- Used **Statsmodels seasonal_decompose** to break down the series into:
  - **Trend** (long-term movement in demand)
  - **Seasonality** (recurring patterns such as monthly cycles)
  - **Residual/Noise** (random fluctuations and anomalies)
- Generated visual plots to clearly separate these components for stakeholder review.

---

## Deliverables
- **Cleaned and indexed dataset** ready for forecasting.
- **Aggregated views** (daily, weekly, monthly) for flexible analysis.
- **Decomposition charts** showing trend, seasonality, and residuals.
- Documentation of preprocessing steps and rationale for chosen methods.

---

## Outcomes
- Established a reliable **data foundation** for demand forecasting.
- Improved dataset quality by handling missing values and ensuring consistent time intervals.
- Provided stakeholders with **visual insights** into demand patterns and anomalies.
- Set the stage for Week 2 tasks (forecast model selection and anomaly detection setup).

---

✅ **Week 1 successfully completed**: The dataset is now structured, decomposed, and ready for forecasting and anomaly detection in the upcoming phases.
