
## 📊 Project Summary: Supply Chain Analytics  
**Goal:** Build a time-series analytics pipeline to forecast demand and detect anomalies in supply chain data.  
**Business Impact:**  
- Prevent overstocking (avoiding waste and tied-up capital).  
- Prevent stockouts (avoiding lost sales and customer dissatisfaction).  
- Detect anomalies early (e.g., sudden demand spikes or supply delays).  

**Key Metrics:**  
- Forecast accuracy → measured by **MAPE** or **RMSE**.  
- Anomaly detection → balance between catching true outliers and avoiding false alerts.  

**User Personas:**  
- **Supply Chain Manager:** Uses forecasts for procurement planning.  
- **Operations Analyst:** Relies on anomaly alerts to investigate disruptions.  

**MVP Deliverable:**  
An interactive **Streamlit web app** where stakeholders can:  
- Select product categories.  
- View 90-day demand forecasts.  
- See historical anomalies highlighted on charts.
