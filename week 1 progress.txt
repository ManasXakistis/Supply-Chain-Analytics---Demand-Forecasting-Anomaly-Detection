# Week 1 Summary – Time-Series Preprocessing & Decomposition

## Objective

Established a reliable foundation for the demand forecasting pipeline by preparing, cleaning, and analyzing the time-series dataset to support accurate forecasting and anomaly detection.

## Activities Completed

### 1. Dataset Preparation

* Collected historical supply chain data containing daily sales and inventory records.
* Validated data quality by checking for missing dates, duplicate records, and inconsistent formats.

### 2. Time-Series Processing

* Converted the `date` column into a datetime index and organized the data chronologically.
* Resampled the dataset into daily, weekly, and monthly views to enable analysis at different time granularities.

### 3. Data Cleaning

* Identified missing observations and handled them using appropriate interpolation and forward-fill techniques.
* Ensured a complete and consistent time series for further analysis.

### 4. Time-Series Decomposition

* Applied seasonal decomposition to separate the data into **Trend**, **Seasonality**, and **Residual** components.
* Generated visualizations to better understand demand patterns and underlying variations.

## Deliverables

* Cleaned and structured time-series dataset.
* Daily, weekly, and monthly aggregated datasets.
* Trend, seasonality, and residual decomposition visualizations.
* Documentation of preprocessing methodology.

## Outcomes

* Built a high-quality dataset ready for forecasting and anomaly detection.
* Improved data consistency and reliability through preprocessing.
* Identified key demand patterns to support model development in the next phase.

**✅ Week 1 Completed:** The dataset has been successfully prepared and is ready for forecasting model development and anomaly detection in Week 2.
